The stars aligned for us to have a Halloween on a Saturday this year.
I was pretty set on becoming Tung Tung Tung Sahur *months* before the day,
and it surprised me how easy it was to execute.

![Me in costume on the street](./tung.jpg)
![Group photo](./group.jpg)

Tung Tung Tung Sahur is some AI-generated character that I adore because 
he looks so stupid and frankly, creepy as well. People on the street mostly
called me "Triple T". So cool!

![The original Tung Tung Tung Sahur](./tung_original.png)

I don't mean to brag, but it's times like these that I can consider myself a true 
engineer. There were some constraints I worked with: I had to ensure I could 
get in and out of the costume, that I could see, and that it wouldn't get loose 
while wearing it.

Having an arm hole as the "locking" mechanism solved two of those problems, rather 
elegantly I must say. I happened to get the arm hole and eye socket placements 
right the first time as well. I just wish I could've made the face larger, but 
I'm very happy with the end product, which in truth took no more than an hour in total.

For the rest of the costume, I had a huge roll from the cloth I bought for 
[last year's costume](../2025-11-halloween/) as the stick, and I had some Vibram
FiveFinger shoes to look "barefoot". Oh, and some years-old paint.

Underneath the costume, I wore a black shirt and shorts, and rolled them up to hide them,
which I think really sold the costume. Nothing creepier than a naked-looking
guy with thunder thighs running right at you...

Naturally, I couldn't really see, hear, or talk much, but I kinda enjoyed the 
anonymity and sensory deprivation.

![Us in costumes at Orchard road](./orchardroad.jpg)

I'm just wondering what I'll do next year. We're already looking forward to it.
Enjoy the photos!
