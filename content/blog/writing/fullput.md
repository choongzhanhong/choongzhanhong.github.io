Rami[^rami] was the Safety Officer on the mission which in retrospect required 
none, which was a strange thing to observe.

And there was another thing he wasn't sure about. The crew members liked to use 
the phrase "Full Put"[^fullput] in just about any context he could recall.

---

This story is based on another dream I had on the night of 14 October. 
I decided maybe I should try to put
some of the dreams into writing and format them into something that makes sense,
although it's eerie that the way they presented themselves to my dreaming self,
they were already rather well-structured, and I hardly had anything to change.

[^rami]: In my dream, this character was portrayed by Rami Malek or a likeness.
[^fullput]: I recall this phrase being used in the dream and it stuck. It's by my 
own transformation that I turned this into a code word for the sake of the story.
