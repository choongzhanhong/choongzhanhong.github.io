### Costs

Costs listed are the total prices.

- Flights: SGD 1014 (Emirates, SIN to DXB to TIA)
- Car Rental:
    - Rental: 379 SGD (EUR 258.64)
    - Gas: SGD 181
    - Cross-border fee: SGD 148 (EUR 50 per country)
    - North Macedonia green card: SGD 74 (EUR 50)
- Accommodations: SGD 721.26
- Food: SGD 318

#### Total

We split the costs over 2 pax, so the total cost paid by each of us was somewhere
in the range of $2000 to $2500. For me, I spent a bit more on beers and souvenirs,
so my costs might be closer to $2500.

Between the three countries, I think Albania is the most expensive, followed by North Macedonia,
then Kosovo. The prices weren't too cheap, granted we spent most of our time in 
touristy places. 

It depends on what you're buying. A beer and pizza in a tourist 
spot like Theth is still going to be cheap and fulfilling. Gas is definitely cheaper outside of Albania.
